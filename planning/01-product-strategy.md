# 01 — Product Strategy

## Niche (decided)

**Dark Psychology / Manipulation.**

Chosen over "women psychology" and "mental peace" because:

- Highest curiosity trigger → strong replays and shares.
- Evergreen, cross-gender, wide audience ceiling.
- Natural monetization: self-help apps, books, courses, plus a future
  in-house psychology / persuasion mini-course.
- Shorts → long-form funnel works (45s hook pulls subs into 12-min deep dives).
- Better CPM + shareability + course-selling combo than the alternatives.

### Adjacent topic bank (for future content / spin-offs)
Cognitive biases · narcissism & toxic people · behavioral economics · power
dynamics · childhood trauma & adult behavior · social anxiety · high-value
mindset · emotional intelligence.

## Audience & Language (decided)

- **Language:** English (NOT Bengali — Bengali/South-Asia RPM is ~10x lower).
- **Target geo:** USA / EU.

## Revenue Model

AdSense is the floor, not the goal. Real upside = course sales + sponsorships
layered on top (estimated 3x–5x AdSense).

### RPM reference (dark psychology niche)

| Audience | RPM | Earnings / 10k views (7–10 min) |
|----------|-----|----------------------------------|
| USA/UK/CA | $3–$8 | $30–$80 |
| Mixed global | $1.5–$4 | $15–$40 |
| South Asia heavy | $0.3–$0.8 | $3–$8 |

## Targets

Goal: **$1000/month from AdSense alone** at ~$5 average RPM (USA/EU).

- Required: **~200,000 views/month**, sustained.
- Cadence: **3 videos/week (12/month)** → **~17,000 views/video**.
- Subscriber base needed: **~30k–50k** (only 5–15% of subs watch each video).

### Realistic timeline (consistent English output)

| Month | Position |
|-------|----------|
| 1–3 | 0–500 subs, testing hooks/thumbnails |
| 4–6 | 1k–5k subs, first viral video possible |
| 7–12 | 10k–30k subs |
| 12–18 | 30k–50k subs, $500–$1000 AdSense range |

Adding one $27 mini-course can pull the $1000/month mark forward to ~month 8–10.

## What actually decides success (priority order)

1. **Thumbnail** — ~60% of the outcome. Dark, high contrast, one emotive face / bold claim.
2. **Title** — must open a knowledge gap ("Why smart people get manipulated easily").
3. **First 30 seconds** — hard hook or retention collapses and the algo kills it.
4. **Avg view duration** — needs ~40–50%+ to keep getting pushed.
5. **Visual/audio differentiation** — see [03-variation-system.md](03-variation-system.md).

> Format risk: YouTube penalizes low-effort faceless content. Generic
> Pexels + basic text + AI voice struggles past ~500 views. The system must
> look directed, not assembled. The realistic, emotional AI voice is the
> single biggest built-in advantage — everything else is built to match it.
